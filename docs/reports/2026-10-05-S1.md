How the fix works, in short:
- **Affordability.** Double/Split need: (every bet on the table) + (one more bet of the active hand) ≤ In-Play. This is checked in the flags and again inside the actions.
- **Round ownership.** A `roundId` counter moves on at every deal and at every leave. Each delayed callback (the dealer pause, settlement, the auto-return) captures `{sessionId, roundId}` and does nothing if either has changed.
- **One money operation at a time.**
  - Settlement, re-buy, Buy-in and leaving run one at a time.
  - A repeated re-buy is ignored.
  - Leaving, settling and Buy-in wait for the running operation, then look at the state again. So a duplicate finds nothing to do, and a leave is never dropped.
- **Phase guards.** Hit/Stand/Double/Split act only in `playerTurn`. `startRound` acts only in `betting`. Cancel, open-sheet and insurance act only from their own phase.
- **Action lockout.** After the buttons under the finger change, every player action is ignored for a while (section 4). Leaving is never locked.
