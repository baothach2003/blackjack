## How findings are triaged

A finding **blocks** further feature work when it gives a wrong money amount,
a wrong hand outcome, or leaves the player stuck, AND it is reachable in
normal play. A finding that needs a rules or product decision first is
**decide**. Everything else is **later** (cleanup, polish, docs). This rule
lives in `CLAUDE.md` section 12 and applies to every future session.

## Reproductions

Each was a temporary Jest test against the real store with a fixed deck,
asserting the current (wrong) behaviour; all four passed, so all four bugs are
real. The fix session turns each into a regression test asserting the correct
behaviour.

- **A1.** Buy in 100. Hand 6+5, bet 100, dealer 10+8, next card 2. `double()`:
  hand 13 loses 200, In-Play = -100. After `SETTLEMENT_PAUSE_MS`,
  `screenPhase` is `'betting'` (not `'buyInSheet'`). `confirmLeaveTable()`
  rejects with "Credited amount cannot be negative"; `session` stays open.
- **A2.** Buy in 500, stand on 19 vs 18, let it settle, `confirmLeaveTable()`
  (now `'idle'`, no session). Advance `SETTLEMENT_PAUSE_MS`: `screenPhase`
  becomes `'betting'` with `session === null`.
- **R1.** Two split hands [A,K] and [A,9], bet 50 each, dealer 10+8.
  `resultQueue[0]` is `{ result: 'blackjack', amount: 75 }`.
- **R2.** Buy in 500, stand on 20 vs dealer 17. During `'dealerTurn'` call
  `confirmLeaveTable()`. Balance ends at 950: the won hand was recorded as a
  loss.
